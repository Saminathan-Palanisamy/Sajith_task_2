## **6.0​ Compliance**


6.1​ SOP review cadence


6.1.1​ This SOP shall be reviewed biennially.
6.1.2​ This SOP shall also be reviewed upon significant changes to Inmar’s business,

regulatory, and/or technical environment that could impact the content of this
document.

6.2​ Compliance Measurement


6.2.1​ The Information Security Governance, Risk, and Compliance team will verify

compliance to this SOP through various methods, including but not limited to,
business tool reports, internal and external audits, and feedback to the author.
6.3​ Sanctions


6.3.1​ Inmar’s sanction policy applies appropriate sanctions, up to and including

termination, against Inmar team members who fail to comply with Inmar’s
Information Security Policies and Procedures. Sanctions occur with appropriate
involvement of Inmar’s human resources and legal departments.
